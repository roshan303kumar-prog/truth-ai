How TRUTH AI Works

A multi-stage pipeline designed to parse claims, interrogate independent knowledge bases, audit media compression, and construct an evidence-grounded report.

1

Claim Ingestion & Linguistic Parsing

When a text claim is submitted, the natural language engine performs tokenization, removes non-informational stopwords, and isolates core factual propositions (e.g. subject, predicate, claimed action, dates, numerical values).

  • Sensationalism Scan: Evaluates capitalization ratios and sensationalist markers designed to manipulate emotional reactions.
  • Entity Extraction: Identifies institutions, public figures, geographic entities, and referenced dates.
2

Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval

Rather than trusting a single data source, TRUTH AI queries independent evidence providers simultaneously via its modular adapter interface:

Google Fact Check: ClaimReview schema from certified IFCN fact-checkers.
DuckDuckGo: Real-time public web indexing and news abstracts.
Wikipedia Reference: Established historical, scientific, and encyclopedic consensus.
Curated Ground Truth: Peer-reviewed database for contemporary scientific consensus.
3

Stance Alignment & Contradiction Detection

Each retrieved citation is classified into one of four stance relationships relative to the submitted claim:

Supporting: Corroborates the event, statistics, and causal claims. Contradicting: Authoritative reporting directly disputes or debunks the assertion. Misleading / Missing Context: True premise combined with distorted framing or omitted baseline data. Contextual / Neutral: Related background reporting that neither confirms nor denies the core assertion.
4

Optical & Forensic Media Processing

For photograph and video uploads, specialized computer vision routines audit visual integrity:

  • Error Level Analysis (ELA): Re-compresses the image at 90% JPEG quality to highlight digital splicing and composited elements.
  • Metadata & EXIF Audit: Extracts camera hardware sensor tags and flags the presence of editing software (e.g. Photoshop, GIMP).
  • AI Synthesis Detection: Analyzes diffusion generator prompt signatures (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney) and frequency-domain spectral characteristics.
  • Keyframe Temporal Video Audit: Evaluates inter-frame focal sharpness and lighting stability across OpenCV-extracted frames.
5

Explainable Synthesis & Limitations Disclosure

The engine compiles the final verdict, confidence score, and clear narrative explanation. Every report explicitly lists methodologic limitations, reminding the user that automated systems are tools to assist human inquiry, not infallible arbiters of truth.
