About TRUTH AI

An advanced multi-modal fake news detection and media forensic platform designed to bridge algorithmic natural language processing with human critical thinking.

Project Genesis & Objective

The digital information ecosystem is saturated with synthetic media, manipulated quotes, and viral disinformation. Conventional fake news classifiers frequently commit two critical flaws: they rely on simplistic, easily fooled keyword lists, or they act as uninterpretable black boxes that state a claim is "100% fake" without citing supporting or refuting documentation.

TRUTH AI was architected from the ground up to uphold the principle of epistemic honesty. Finding a matching search result does not prove a claim is authentic; conversely, missing search results do not prove a claim is fabricated. Instead, TRUTH AI evaluates multiple independent sources, compares supporting and contradicting stances, and transparently articulates uncertainty and limitations.

The 4 Architectural Pillars

Multi-Source Triangulation

Connects across Google Fact Check Tools API, DuckDuckGo web search, and Wikipedia reference graphs. Aggregates and cross-validates claims across independent institutional databases.

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

In photo forensics, computes localized JPEG compression variance across 8x8 pixel blocks. Digitally altered or spliced elements exhibit distinct error levels compared to surrounding natural content.

Temporal Video Continuity

Extracts uniform keyframe sequences with OpenCV. Assesses Laplacian focal sharpness variance, frame-to-frame lighting transitions, and on-screen chyron text through OCR.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Generates clear, plain-language explanations explaining precisely why a conclusion was reached, citing specific sources, dates, and discrepancies without hallucinating unobserved facts.
